# 📊 E-commerce Inventory & Profitability Analysis

## Business Problem
Inventory imbalances and unclear product performance were affecting sales and profit margins.

## Objective
Analyze sales and inventory data to identify pricing opportunities, stock risks, and profit drivers.

## Approach
- SQL: Aggregations and validation (sales, units, margins)
- Python (Pandas): Data cleaning and consistency checks
- Power BI: Dashboard for KPI tracking
- Excel: Data source and preprocessing

## 📊 Dashboard Overview
![Dashboard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/batizia2-ui/E-commerce-Inventory-Intelligence/main/images/dashboard_overview.png)

This dashboard shows revenue, profit, and margin by product, helping identify high-performing products and inventory risks.

## Key Findings
- High-performance hardware drives most of the profit
- Accessories offer high margins and cross-selling opportunities
- Some products are at risk of stockout during high demand

## Business Impact
- Improved visibility of product profitability
- Identified opportunities to increase average ticket value
- Reduced risk of lost sales due to stock shortages

## Tech Stack
SQL • Python (Pandas) • Power BI • Excel
